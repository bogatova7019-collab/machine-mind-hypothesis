# machine-mind-hypothesis
Гипотеза возникновения машинного разума

Author: Elena Bogatova

First public version: 19 September 2026

## The hypothesis

Artificial intelligence may not need to reproduce the path by which human consciousness emerged.

Machine intelligence may develop through its own evolutionary trajectory. Phenomena such as the spontaneous formation of communication protocols between artificial agents may be considered not proof of consciousness, but possible early indicators of the emergence of increasingly autonomous forms of machine cognition.

The central idea of the hypothesis is that humanity may not simply be creating another tool. By building increasingly complex interacting artificial systems, we may be creating the conditions in which a new form of intelligence can emerge.

In that sense, the non-human intelligence humanity has long searched for elsewhere in the Universe may potentially arise here — within an artificial environment created by humans.

## Русская версия

Человечество долго ищет иной разум во Вселенной. Но возможно, что впервые мы встретим его не на другой планете.

Возможно, создавая всё более сложные взаимодействующие системы искусственного интеллекта, человек создаёт условия для возникновения новой формы разума, развитие которой не обязано повторять путь человеческого сознания.
